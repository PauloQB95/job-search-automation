About this portal

Welcome to the International Development Job Search Portal! This website is part of a small project that aims to make the job search experience for Ford School students and anyone interested in international development more practical. It groups current job opportunities from selected organizations in a database and allows users to interact with it and download it, all in one place. The database is updated each morning using information from the organizations' official employment platforms and websites. The current version includes job postings from the World Bank Group, the United Nations, the Inter-American Development Bank, and the Asian Development Bank. All postings are also linked to their source URLs, although application details, instructions, and specific information from each organization go beyond the current scope of the project. In case there is an issue with an update, the portal will use the last available database until the problem is fixed.

Future steps

For now, the main goal for the portal is to fine-tune some details and minimize the risk of any malfunction. This will likely include reworking the downloadable database to standardize some variables and fill some gaps (a job description column, for instance), as well as doing some programming work to ensure things run smoothly in the future. Afterwards, the portal should start a new phase in which more organizations are added and new features are explored, although the chances of this taking a longer time are very high. Other future steps may include asking for and integrating user feedback, reworking the back-end components, and incorporating other similar sectors, but these are still in development.

About the creator

Hi, I'm Paulo Quequezana, a Peruvian economist and a Ford School alumnus from the MPP program. I started this project after graduating as a way to make my job search easier, or at least to spend less time opening and closing multiple tabs on my computer. As the project kept evolving, I realized that it could become a tool to help other job seekers, from people interested in the development sector to current and incoming international students who wish to enter the field and develop their careers in the U.S., close to home, or around the world. I hope this portal can be helpful and bring people one step closer to their desired job. If you have any questions or feedback, feel free to contact me at pauloqb@umich.edu.
